AnyFileFormat: PDF & File Tools – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026. Developer: DIY-Automate. Package: dev.anyfileformat.

Summary

AnyFileFormat converts, edits and scans files on your phone. Your files are processed on the device and are not uploaded to the developer. The developer runs no servers and collects no personal data. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some data as described below.

Your files

  • Files you open, convert, edit, scan or merge are processed on the device.
  • Results are saved to the Download/AnyFileFormat folder on your phone. Files you delete in the app go to the phone's Bin (Android 11 and later) or are deleted straight away (older Android).
  • The app reads only the files you choose or share to it. It has no access to your other files.
  • Files leave the device only when you send them yourself, for example with Share, Save to Drive, Gmail or WhatsApp. That app's own privacy policy then applies.

On-device features from Google

Text recognition (OCR), face blurring, background removal and the document scanner use Google ML Kit and Google Play services. They process images on the device. The background-removal model and the document scanner are delivered by Google Play services, which may send Google basic device and app information to provide them, under Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

HTML to PDF and HTML to image

When you enter a web address, the app loads that page like a browser does. The website receives your IP address and standard request data under its own privacy policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner, full-screen and rewarded ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), the app asks for your consent first using Google's consent form, and you can change your choice later from "Ad privacy choices" in the app. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. See how Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Security

Files you work on stay on your device. Data sent by the AdMob SDK and Google Play services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
